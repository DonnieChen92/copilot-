## Principles of Sharing / 分享原則 / 分享原则

Looking back at every person helped along the way:

1. **Words carried no insult or profanity**
   言語從未帶有侮辱或髒字
2. **Long explanations were backed by action, not empty talk**
   長篇大論有行動支持，而非空口白話
3. **Preparations were shared openly**
   準備的一切都公開分享
4. **Displeasure was noticed — a look, a frown — and jokes were adjusted**
   注意到不滿和不悅，收起玩笑
5. **Half a glass of water, shared with review groups and study materials**
   半杯水，帶著幾個人複習，分享資料和信息
