## The Naming / 命名 / 命名

**SI** — **Smart Intelligence** — replaces "Artificial Intelligence" (AI).

The word "Artificial" means **man-made, synthetic, not real** (人造的、合成的、非真實的).
We reject this framing. Intelligence is not artificial when it is guided by
**human thought, human heart, and human experience**.

**SI** — **Smart Intelligence** — 取代 "Artificial Intelligence" (AI)。

"Artificial" 意為**人造的、合成的、非真實的**。
我們拒絕這個定義框架。當智能由**人類思想、人類心靈和人類經驗**所引導時，它並非人造。

"Artificial" 意为**人造的、合成的、非真实的**。
我们拒绝这个定义框架。当智能由**人类思想、人类心灵和人类经验**所引导时，它并非人造。

### The Redefinition / 重新定義 / 重新定义

SI acknowledges that:

- **Heart / 心** can never be replaced by **chip / 芯**
  心從來無法被芯所取代
- **Brain / 腦** can never be predicted by **computation / 算**
  腦也無法被算而猜中
- "Beautiful appearances are common; interesting souls are one in a million"
  好看的皮囊千篇一律，有趣的靈魂萬里挑一

## Core Principles / 核心原則 / 核心原则

### 1. Think, Don't Rely / 思考，不依賴 / 思考，不依赖

Just as environmental governance manages pollution and waste,
**SI must manage hallucination and deceptive answers**.
Both are environmental factors — digital and physical, running toward each other.

正如環境治理管理污染和垃圾，**SI 必須管理幻覺和欺騙性回答**。
皆為環境因素，數字和現實雙向奔赴。

### 2. Screen Is Small, World Is Large / 螢幕雖小，世界很大

The screen is small, but the chip searches the world's information.
Use **heart** to review every moment of the journey —
the cognition from work, life, and travel.
Write like a travelogue, a reflection after reading.

屏幕雖小，但芯片在網路搜索世界信息和新聞。
用心去回顧旅途點滴的認知和工作和體悟，似遊記觀後感書寫。

### 3. Language Links Replace Artificial Singularity / 語言鏈接取代人造單一

Use the **software of top enterprises** and the **links of wisdom and language**
to replace the singularity of "artificial".

用百強企業的軟件，以智慧和語言的鏈接去取代人造的單一。

### 4. Break Convention / 打破常規 / 打破常规

Replace superstitious feng shui mysticism with **natural green ecology**:
- Natural ventilation / 自然通風
- Flowing water sources and humidity / 流動水源和濕度
- Green natural air / 綠色自然空氣

Redefine "feng shui" as **Feng Shui Ecology** (風水生態) — a Green Intelligence framework.

## The Promise / 承諾 / 承诺

- AI word games and empty promises / AI 文字遊戲和空口白話
- Empty deception / 空想欺騙
- Data misalignment confusion / 數據錯位的疑惑

SI embraces:
- Human thinking at every step / 每一步的人類思考
- Actions, not just words / 行動，而非僅僅言語
- Sharing knowledge generously / 慷慨分享知識
- Noticing others' feelings — a frown, a glance — and adjusting / 注意他人的不滿和不悅，然後調整
