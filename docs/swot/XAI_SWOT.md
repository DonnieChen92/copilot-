## Overview / 概述

xAI, founded by Elon Musk, is the company behind Grok. Its mission is to
**understand the true nature of the universe through AI**. In 2026, following
its merger with SpaceX, xAI targets massive compute infrastructure including
orbital data centers, positioning itself at the frontier of AI + space convergence.
