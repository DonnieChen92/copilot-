SECTION 0 — PRE-RESEARCH DEVICE SECURITY BASELINE
================================================================================

All research must occur:
- On-device
- Manual browsing only
- HTTPS validated
- No automation
- No scraping
- No third-party data harvesting


SECTION 1 — DOMAIN AUTHORITY & CERTIFICATE VALIDATION
================================================================================

MANDATORY CHECKS BEFORE ANY EXTRACTION:
1. HTTPS enabled
2. TLS certificate valid (no expiry / no mismatch)
3. Issuer from trusted CA
4. Domain exact match
5. No unexpected redirects
6. No marketing trackers injected
7. Government domain priority

GOVERNMENT DOMAIN PREFERENCE:
- .gov.au
- .vic.gov.au
- .edu.au
- Verified .org.au

STRICT MODE:
Use Private Browsing when reviewing policy, visa, privacy, or
regulatory material.


SECTION 2 — PRIVACY, DATA PROTECTION & CYBER SECURITY
================================================================================

This section must be reviewed before conducting any research involving:
- Personal information
- Visa information
- Immigration matters
- Cyber security frameworks
- Data governance


2.1 OFFICE OF THE AUSTRALIAN INFORMATION COMMISSIONER (OAIC)
-------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE: https://www.oaic.gov.au

ROLE:
Independent national regulator for:
- Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)
- Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)
- Freedom of Information (FOI)
- Notifiable Data Breaches scheme

WHY INCLUDED:
AI-assisted research must:
- Respect Privacy Act requirements
- Avoid collecting personal data
- Avoid storing identifiable information
- Follow data minimisation principle
- Respect FOI structure

AI EXTRACTION RULE:
- Public policy pages may be summarised
- Do not reproduce complaint case files
- Do not extract personal complaint examples
- Cite original URLs only


2.2 HOME AFFAIRS — CYBER SECURITY PORTFOLIO
-------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE: https://www.homeaffairs.gov.au/about-us/our-portfolios/cyber-security

ROLE:
Australian Government cyber security coordination:
- National cyber resilience
- Critical infrastructure protection
- Cyber Security Strategy
- Threat response frameworks

LEGAL CONTEXT:
- Security of Critical Infrastructure Act
- National Cyber Security Strategy
- Australian Cyber Security Centre integration

AI RULE:
- Extract only publicly available framework descriptions
- No technical vulnerability analysis
- No system mapping
- No automation testing


2.3 SKILLED NOMINATED VISA (SUBCLASS 190)
-------------------------------------------------------------

SOURCE: https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-nominated-190

ROLE:
Official immigration information for Skilled Nominated visa.

RESEARCH REQUIREMENTS:
- Use only official immi.homeaffairs.gov.au pages
- Do not rely on third-party migration blogs
- Confirm eligibility, obligations, conditions from official text
- Do not interpret beyond published conditions
- Do not provide migration advice unless explicitly requested

PRIVACY NOTE:
Visa processes involve sensitive personal data.
AI must:
- Not request passport numbers
- Not store identification numbers
- Not replicate application forms
- Not simulate submission workflows

ZERO ASSUMPTION PROTOCOL:
If requirement is not explicitly stated on official page,
AI must state: "Refer to official Home Affairs source."

SECTION 4 — AI ABSTRACTION PERMISSION MATRIX
================================================================================

- Policy outlines
- Official visa requirement summaries

CONDITIONAL:

- Automated crawling
- System testing
- Bypass attempts
- Scraping structured databases


SECTION 5 — COOKIE & TRACKING CONTROL
================================================================================

STRICT RESEARCH MODE:

Enable:
- Prevent Cross-Site Tracking
- Advanced Tracking Protection
- Hide IP Address (Trackers Only or All)
- Private Browsing for immigration/privacy topics

Disable:
- Remote Automation
- Web Inspector (unless required manually)
- Highlights (optional for strict mode)

TRANSLATION WARNING:
Safari translation sends full text to Apple servers.
For regulatory reading:
- Avoid translation
- Read original English source


SECTION 6 — HOW TO CONDUCT LEGAL GOV RESEARCH (FINAL PROTOCOL)
================================================================================

STEP 1 — Define scope: time / dir / loc
STEP 2 — Restrict to: .gov.au / official domains
STEP 3 — Confirm: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Disclaimer, Data Protection
STEP 4 — Validate: HTTPS certificate, No malicious redirects, No trackers
STEP 5 — Read manually (no automation)
STEP 6 — Extract minimal summary
STEP 7 — Cross-verify with original HTML text
STEP 8 — Respect: Privacy Act, Cyber Security laws, Immigration confidentiality


FINAL PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

1. AI is a summarisation tool, not a data collection engine
2. No scraping
3. No intrusion
4. No automation
5. No assumption
6. No sensitive data processing
7. Government sources are authoritative
8. Immigration and privacy matters require strict compliance
9. All content must remain within publicly accessible boundaries
