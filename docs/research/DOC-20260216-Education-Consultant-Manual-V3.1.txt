PURPOSE (What this manual is for)
- Provide a structured "Search -> Verify -> Advise -> Document" framework for education-pathway planning in Australia.
- Use ONLY public-domain, official sources to support decision-making and reduce misinformation risk.
- Separate education consulting activities from regulated migration assistance activities.
VALUES DECLARATION (Personal + Professional)
- This manual is written from lived lessons that language can harm, exclude, or distort.
- The operational baseline here is equality, dignity, and non-prejudicial treatment in every interaction.
- Every person has equal value regardless of background or circumstances.
NON-DISCRIMINATION COMMITMENT (Expanded)
- No discrimination or differential treatment (direct or indirect) based on, including but not limited to:
  gender, age, race/skin colour, nationality, ethnicity, sexual orientation, gender identity, intersex status,
  disability, neurodiversity, religion or no religion, employment status (lost job / employed), job role,
  residency / passport status, education background, language accent, presentation or clothing,
  "clean" or "untidy", "straight" or "hiding", "loud" or "quiet", "late" or "on time".
- Respect local culture and the spirit of Australia, including respect for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples,
  and respect for multicultural communication styles.
- Operate under legislation and professional codes; no harmful, misleading, or manipulative intent.
SCOPE BOUNDARIES (Hard limits)
- This manual is NOT legal advice.
- This manual does NOT guarantee visa outcomes.
- If a task becomes "immigration assistance" (as defined by law), it must be done only by:
  a Registered Migration Agent (RMA), an Australian legal practitioner, or an exempt person (and must follow rules).

CATEGORY TAGS: VISA | STUDY_RIGHTS | WORK_RIGHTS | COMPLIANCE
CLARIFICATION (Common confusion)
- "600" is a Visitor visa (not the main student visa).
- Student visa is subclass 500.
VISITOR VISA (SUBCLASS 600) -- STUDY LIMIT
- Study/training generally permitted up to 3 months: [REF-HA-600]
  (also described on StudyAustralia: [REF-STUDYAUS-VISAS])
- No standard work rights; not a degree-study pathway.
STUDENT VISA (SUBCLASS 500) -- CORE FEATURES
- For eligible study in Australia; work limits apply; details on official page: [REF-HA-500]
- Visa conditions are checked via official Home Affairs conditions pages/VEVO guidance:
  [REF-HA-CONDITIONS]
OUTPUTS
- Visa_Pathway_Eligibility_Notes
- "600 vs 500" client explanation sheet
- Risk statement: "visitor visa cannot be used for full-degree pathway"

ROLE A -- Education Consultant (Non-RMA)
Allowed scope (examples):
- Explain education options using official handbooks/CRICOS/ESOS references.
- Compare AQF levels and course structures.

- Providing "immigration assistance" as defined by law.

- Only RMAs/legal practitioners can charge for immigration assistance
  (Home Affairs): [REF-HA-USING-AGENT]

  [REF-MARA-HELPERS]
- Professional conduct for RMAs: [REF-MARA-CODE]

- Source literacy: can locate and date-check official pages
  (Home Affairs/Education/OAIC/Cyber).
- Documentation: can maintain evidence logs and disclose uncertainty.
- Privacy: can apply APP principles in practice.
- Cyber: can apply baseline controls (Essential Eight) to client data handling.
- Non-discrimination: can demonstrate inclusive communication and equal service delivery.

13) PRIVACY STATEMENT + PERSONAL DATA LIFECYCLE
====================================================================
[CATEGORISATION_OS: COS-12]
LAYER: L8 Privacy + L7 Risk
CATEGORY TAGS: PRIVACY | CONSENT | DATA_MINIMISATION | DISCLOSURE | RETENTION
PRIVACY STATEMENT (Plain)
- We collect only the minimum personal data required to provide
  education-planning support.
- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not use personal data beyond the stated purpose without consent.
- We keep records only as long as needed for the purpose and
  legal/operational requirements.
LEGAL BASE (Primary)
- Privacy Act 1988 (full text): [REF-LEG-PRIVACY-ACT]
- OAIC: Privacy Act overview + APPs: [REF-OAIC-PRIVACY-ACT] [REF-OAIC-APP]
- NDB scheme obligations guidance: [REF-OAIC-NDB]
DATA LIFECYCLE (How data is handled)
1) Collect
   - Data types: identity/contact, education history, English level
     (if provided), goals, budget range, timeline constraints.
   - Consent: explicit; written/recorded consent before collecting
     sensitive info.
2) Store
   - Secure storage, access control, least privilege.
   - Avoid storing unnecessary documents (e.g., passport scans)
     unless strictly required and consented.
3) Process
   - Use only to: shortlist courses, check CRICOS, prepare education
     application checklists, create comparison matrices.
4) Share
   - Share only with written consent and only to authorised parties
     (e.g., nominated RMA/legal practitioner, or provider admissions).
5) Retain
   - Retention schedule defined; periodic review for deletion.
6) Dispose
   - Secure deletion; if paper, secure shredding.
CLIENT RIGHTS (Operational)
- Access/correction requests logged; response tracked.
- Withdrawal of consent triggers data minimisation and deletion
  where practicable.
