2. APPENDIX SYSTEM (Methodology Modules V1.0)
================================================================================


Appendix A — Source Authority Hierarchy (1-6)
------------------------------------------------------

Level | Category                        | Typical Examples
------|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------
  1   | Government & Legislative Bodies  | vic.gov.au, legislation.vic.gov.au,
      |                                 | Gazette, LUV, CAV, ABS
  2   | Official / Statutory Bodies      | DataVic, ACSC/cyber.gov.au, NASC,
      |                                 | Local Government Victoria
  3   | Company Official (with ABN)     | ABR/ABN Lookup verifiable .com.au/.org.au
  4   | Academic & Research             | .edu.au universities, research centres
  5   | Mainstream Media                | ABC, The Age, Herald Sun, Guardian AU
  6   | Community / User Reviews        | Maps reviews, forums, social media

In scoring system, Level determines SourceAuthority base score range.


Appendix B — Composite Trust Scoring Model
------------------------------------------------------

Formula:
  T = 0.4 x SourceAuthority
    + 0.2 x Transparency
    + 0.2 x EvidenceQuality
    + 0.1 x Security
    + 0.1 x CrossCheck

Each dimension 1-5 scale.

Engineering implementation: can be a rule function, or in vector
retrieval / RAG pipeline as re-ranking additional weight.


Appendix C — Website Safety 5-Point Check (Technical)
------------------------------------------------------

1. HTTPS & Certificate
2. Domain Registration Entity & Policy Constraints
3. Primary Domain vs Lookalike Domain
4. Redirect Behaviour (Redirect Hygiene)
5. Browser / Platform Security Alerts + Stop. Check. Protect


Appendix D — Search & Evaluation Pipeline (Orchestratable)
------------------------------------------------------

Phase 1: Government-first Query (site:vic.gov.au etc.)
Phase 2: Regulatory & Cyber Security Layer (CAV, ACCC, Scamwatch, NASC, ACSC)
Phase 3: Business Entity / ABN Verification
Phase 4: Media & Community Feedback
Phase 5: Scoring & Decision (T >= 4 threshold for "trusted fact")


Appendix E — Community / User Content Usage Rules
------------------------------------------------------

Position: Experience feedback, pain point collection, early risk signal.
NOT for establishing legal responsibility, rights/obligations, or
official standards alone.

Priority: Posts that cite & link Level 1-2 official sources.
Downweight: Extreme emotion, pure subjective judgment, no evidence/links,
suspected review flooding.
