[CERTIFICATE VERIFICATION INSTRUCTION]
1. Access official HTTPS domain.
2. Open browser security panel → View certificate details.
3. Record:
   - Issuer Organisation
   - Issuer Country
   - Valid From
   - Valid To
4. Cross-check via Certificate Transparency search:
   https://api.certspotter.com/v1/issuances?domain=DOMAIN
5. Archive verification record with timestamp and reviewer identity.
6. Schedule re-verification prior to expiry.
