SECTION 1 — GOVERNMENT DOMAIN ELIGIBILITY & LEGAL BASIS
================================================================================

WHAT
  Government domain rules & legal basis for confirming source legitimacy
  and authority.

WHERE
  https://www.domainname.gov.au
  https://www.auda.org.au
  https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au
  https://www.legislation.gov.au

WHY
  Ensure visited websites belong to legitimate government or regulated
  entities. Prevent entering lookalike/impersonation domains.

HOW (LEGAL RULES)
  - .gov.au restricted to government entities only
  - Per gov.au domain policy and auDA regulatory framework
  - Legislative text must come from official legislation portals

AI ABSTRACTION PERMISSION CHECK
  - Government websites generally allow reading & citing public info
  - Check Terms of Use & Copyright pages
  - No bulk copying of copyright-protected content; citation & summary only

CONNECTION SECURITY
  - Must be HTTPS
  - Certificate issued by trusted CA
  - Browser lock indicator normal
  - No abnormal redirects
  - No loading of third-party tracking scripts (check DevTools Network)


SECTION 2 — PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION
================================================================================

WHAT
  Privacy statements and data protection rules.

WHERE
  https://www.vic.gov.au/privacy
  https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/privacy
  https://www.cyber.gov.au
  https://www.abs.gov.au/about/privacy

WHY
  AI abstraction and extraction must comply with data minimisation principle.

HOW (LAW & REGULATION)
  - Federal Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)
  - Victorian Privacy and Data Protection Act 2014
  - Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)

AI USAGE RULE
  - Process only public information
  - Do not scrape personal sensitive data
  - Do not reconstruct databases
  - Do not violate robots.txt or Terms

CONNECTION SECURITY CHECK
  - HTTPS enforced
  - Certificate validity check
  - Same-site cookies preferred
  - Government sites typically allow first-party cookies only
  - Block third-party analytics cookies (except official government analytics)

SECTION 7 — CONNECTION SECURITY CERTIFICATE VALIDATION
================================================================================

CHECKLIST
  1. HTTPS required
  2. TLS 1.2+
  3. Valid CA issuer
  4. Certificate matches domain
  5. No mixed content
  6. No suspicious 3rd-party tracking scripts
  7. Government domains preferred

OPTIONAL VALIDATION (Manual Only)
  - Use browser DevTools -> Security tab
  - View Certificate chain
  - View Cookies (First-party only preferred)


SECTION 8 — AI ABSTRACT & EXTRACT PERMISSION MATRIX
================================================================================

ALLOW
  - Public policy summaries
  - Legislative references
  - Public reports data citation
  - Open data download (where permitted)

CONDITIONAL
  - Media articles (respect copyright)
  - NGO annual reports

NOT ALLOWED
  - Paywalled databases
  - Login portals
  - Personal data scraping
  - Bulk copying

ZERO ASSUMPTION RULE
  - Do not fill gaps
  - Do not speculate on future policy
  - Do not generate unverified content

HOW-TO: AI Agent Legal Search Flow
================================================================================

1. Always prioritise .gov.au / .vic.gov.au / .edu.au domains
   Highest authority, no paywall, secure HTTPS, no infringing content

2. Confirm document type hierarchy: L1->L6
   L1 Legislation & Regulations
   L2 Gazette Official Notices
   L3 Government Policy / Official Reports
   L4 Institutional Descriptive Pages
   L5 Press Releases
   L6 User Comments (lowest authority)

3. Check privacy, disclaimers, terms of use:
   Privacy Policy | Data Protection | Disclosure Statements
   Terms of Use | Disclaimer

4. Strictly prohibit retrieval of:
   Paid databases | Infringing materials | Login-required internal platforms
   Unsourced content

5. Allow automated retrieval of:
   All free public websites listed above (all safe, legal, no paywall)

6. Signal priority (AI agent):
   Law > Policy > Public Institutions > Datasets > University/Culture
   > Media > Community

7. All research uses original publishing body as final trusted source.
   AI must NOT infer undisclosed information.
