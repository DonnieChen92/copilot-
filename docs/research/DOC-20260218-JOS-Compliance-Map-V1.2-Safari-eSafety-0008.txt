SECTION 0 — PRE-RESEARCH DEVICE SECURITY BASELINE
================================================================================

SOURCE REVIEWED:
- Safari & Privacy settings (iOS Advanced panel screenshots)
- https://www.esafety.gov.au

OBJECTIVE:
AI-assisted research must operate only after device-level privacy controls
and regulatory awareness are confirmed.


SECTION 1 — SAFARI PRIVACY BASELINE (DEVICE LEVEL)
================================================================================

WHAT (Observed from Screenshots):
- Private Browsing mode
- Intelligent Tracking Prevention
- Hide IP Address
- Privacy Preserving Ad Measurement
- Block All Cookies (toggle)
- Advanced Tracking & Fingerprinting Protection
- JavaScript (enabled)
- Web Inspector (disabled)
- Remote Automation (disabled)

WHY:
Research integrity requires:
- No cross-site tracking
- No third-party cookie leakage
- No browser fingerprinting exposure
- No automated remote scripting
- No hidden relay exploitation

HOW TO CONFIGURE (SECURE MODE):

1. Enable:
   - Prevent Cross-Site Tracking
   - Advanced Tracking & Fingerprinting Protection (All Browsing)
   - Hide IP Address (Trackers Only or All if required)
   - Private Browsing for sensitive research

2. Disable:
   - Remote Automation
   - Web Inspector (unless manual debugging required)
   - Block All Cookies (optional; may break some gov sites)

3. Privacy Preserving Ad Measurement:
   - Can remain enabled (no personal data storage, delayed reporting)

4. Translation Warning:
   - Safari may send full webpage text to Apple servers for translation
   - For sensitive policy reading: DO NOT use translation
   - Read original English source when possible

5. Highlights Feature:
   - May send webpage address via OHTTP
   - For strict research mode: disable Highlights


SECTION 2 — eSAFETY AUSTRALIA COMPLIANCE INTEGRATION
================================================================================

SOURCE:
https://www.esafety.gov.au

ROLE:
The eSafety Commissioner is Australia's independent online safety regulator.

LEGAL CONTEXT:
- Online Safety Act 2021 (Cth)
- Cyber abuse reporting framework
- Image-based abuse regulation
- Removal notices & takedown authority

WHY INCLUDE:
AI-assisted research must:
- Avoid unlawful content
- Avoid harmful dissemination
- Respect online harm reporting structures
- Not reproduce abusive material

HOW TO RESEARCH LEGALLY WITH eSAFETY FRAMEWORK:
1. Verify content classification
2. Avoid copying harmful material
3. Cite regulatory guidance, not user-generated abuse content
4. Respect reporting processes
5. Ensure AI does not amplify prohibited content


SECTION 3 — CONNECTION SECURITY CERTIFICATE CHECK
================================================================================

MANDATORY BEFORE EXTRACTION:
1. Confirm HTTPS
2. Check TLS certificate:
   - Valid issuer
   - No expiry
   - Domain matches
3. No mixed content warnings
4. No unexpected third-party tracking scripts
5. No forced redirects to unrelated domains

GOVERNMENT PREFERENCE:
- .gov.au
- .vic.gov.au
- .edu.au
- Verified .org.au

DO NOT:
- Access login portals
- Bypass authentication
- Use scraping tools
- Execute automation scripts


SECTION 4 — AI ABSTRACTION & EXTRACTION RULES
================================================================================

ALLOWED:
- Summarising public legislation
- Extracting policy headings
- Referencing open datasets
- Citing official URLs

CONDITIONAL:
- NGO annual reports (copyright aware)
- Media summaries (short quotations only)

NOT ALLOWED:
- Bulk copying
- Reproducing paywalled content
- Storing personal data
- Extracting contact databases
- Reverse engineering

ZERO ASSUMPTION PROTOCOL:
- No filling missing gaps
- No predictive claims
- No inferred intentions
- No altered context


SECTION 5 — HOW TO RESEARCH (J.OS MODE WITH SAFETY LAYER)
================================================================================

STEP 1 — Define: time / dir / loc
STEP 2 — Restrict: Official domain whitelist only
STEP 3 — Verify: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Disclaimer, Data Protection
STEP 4 — Validate: HTTPS, Certificate, No third-party trackers
STEP 5 — Read: Manual on-device browsing only
STEP 6 — Extract: Minimal summary, Quote original URL
STEP 7 — Cross-check: Compare with original HTML text
STEP 8 — Confirm: No translation relay, No highlights relay,
                    No unsafe cookie activity


SECTION 6 — COOKIE & THIRD-PARTY CONTROL STANDARD
================================================================================

STRICT MODE:
- Private Browsing ON
- Prevent Cross-Site Tracking ON
- Hide IP Address ON
- Block All Cookies OPTIONAL
- No external analytics tools

ONLY ALLOWED COOKIES:
- First-party session cookies from government domains
- Security authentication cookies for public services

NOT PERMITTED:
- Marketing trackers
- Behavioral profiling cookies
- Embedded advertising scripts
